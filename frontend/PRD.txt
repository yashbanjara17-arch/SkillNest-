# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
Product Name: SkillNest
Tagline: The Right Skills, Right Around You.
Initial Target Market: Boisar, Palghar, Tarapur MIDC, and surrounding regions in Maharashtra.

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
SkillNest is a local-first freelance marketplace designed to connect businesses with verified professionals and teams based on skills, location, portfolio, ratings, and availability.
Unlike global platforms that charge 10-20% commission and disallow in-person coordination, SkillNest allows local business owners (cafe/restaurant owners, gyms, manufacturing units, shopkeepers, local clinics) to discover nearby talent, review local portfolios, contact them directly, and negotiate project prices and milestone terms with 0% platform transaction fees.

2. TARGET AUDIENCE & PERSONAS
A. Clients / Business Owners (Primary non-technical focus):
   - Local cafe, restaurant, bakery, and food outlet owners needing websites, QR menus, or local SEO.
   - Gym, fitness center, dental clinic, and coaching class owners needing branding and Instagram campaigns.
   - Factory, chemical manufacturing, and machine shop managers in Tarapur MIDC needing inventory software, CAD drawings, or corporate drone videos.
   - Requirement: Simple, plain language, intuitive workflows, visible examples, zero technical jargon.

B. Freelancers & Freelance Teams:
   - Solo developers, designers, videographers, CAD engineers, copywriters.
   - Creative collectives and boutique agencies (e.g., 2-4 member teams).
   - Requirement: Professional workspace to showcase verified local work, discover projects, submit quotes, and manage client inquiries.

C. Platform Administrators:
   - Platform managers responsible for overseeing user growth, moderating project listings, inspecting reports, and ensuring local trust.
   - Requirement: Dedicated, secure administrative console separate from client and freelancer workspaces.

3. ROLE-BASED ACCESS & ROUTE ARCHITECTURE
- Public Routes:
  / - Landing Page with 21st.dev / Sera UI inspired glassmorphism design, hero, search, categories, comparison, workflow.
  /freelancers - Find Freelancers directory with location, skill, availability, rating, and agency filters.
  /freelancer/:id - Detailed Freelancer Profile with verified local portfolio gallery and enquiry modal.
  /projects - Browse open projects with category and budget filters.
  /project/:id - Project details with live local matching recommendations and proposal submission.
  /how-it-works - Interactive workflow breakdown, comparison matrix, and FAQ.
  /login & /signup - Clean authentication with role selection (Client vs Freelancer). (Admin registration is strictly prohibited through public forms).

- Client Routes (Protected):
  /dashboard/client - Client Workspace (Welcome, Post Project shortcut, active projects, inquiries, saved talent).
  /post-project - Multi-step, non-technical project creation wizard with plain-language guidance and live matching preview.
  /client/projects - My Projects with status toggles (Draft, Open, In Discussion, Assigned, Completed).
  /client/messages - Conversations & direct project inquiries.
  /client/saved - Bookmarked freelancers.
  /client/profile - Business profile and contact preferences.

- Freelancer Routes (Protected):
  /dashboard/freelancer - Freelancer Workspace (Profile completion, active proposals, inquiries, portfolio quick-manager).
  /freelancer/applications - Proposal tracker with statuses (Pending, In Discussion, Accepted, Declined).
  /freelancer/portfolio - Portfolio management (add, edit, showcase local project deliveries).
  /freelancer/inquiries - Direct client messages received.
  /freelancer/saved - Saved project opportunities.
  /freelancer/profile - Professional headline, bio, skills, services, rates, availability.

- Admin Routes (Protected, Admin role only):
  /admin - Executive Admin Dashboard (KPIs, role distribution, project activity metrics).
  /admin/users - User management (search, filter by client/freelancer/admin, status toggling).
  /admin/projects - Project moderation and inspection.
  /admin/reviews - Review audit and moderation.
  /admin/settings - System preferences & regional hub parameters.

4. DESIGN SYSTEM: PREMIUM GLASSMORPHISM
- Palette: Deep midnight navy / slate foundations (`#090D16`, `#0F172A`, `#1E293B`), crisp translucent glass layers (`bg-white/70`, `bg-slate-900/60`, `backdrop-blur-xl`), fine hairline borders (`border-white/10`, `border-slate-200/60`), vibrant cyan/violet/indigo accents (`#6366F1`, `#38BDF8`, `#8B5CF6`).
- Readability first: Essential forms and text content maintain WCAG AA contrast (minimum 4.5:1 ratio).
- Non-technical terminology: "What do you need help with?", "Your budget", "Find local experts", "Contact freelancer".

5. BUSINESS MODEL & TERMS
- Direct price negotiation: Price is mutually agreed upon by client and freelancer.
- 0% platform commission on project transactions.
- Future monetization: Optional verified badges, featured promotional placement, and business concierge packages.
